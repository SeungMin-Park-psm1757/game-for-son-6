# ART_PROMPT_LIBRARY

These prompts are for creating **original** assets for a light arcade soccer webapp with big-head stylized characters.
Do **not** generate art based on real athletes, real jerseys, or recognizable existing sports-game branding.

---

## 1. Character Base Prompt

Stylized arcade soccer character, oversized head, compact body, tiny limbs, bold outline, playful sports energy, original fictional athlete, no resemblance to any real person, no team logo, no national flag, readable silhouette, colorful but clean, mobile-game friendly, transparent background

### Additions by archetype
- striker: dynamic pose, aggressive eyes, forward lean
- defender: sturdy stance, strong jaw, wider body block
- trickster: sly smile, asymmetrical pose, playful energy
- speedster: sharp hair shape, motion streak styling
