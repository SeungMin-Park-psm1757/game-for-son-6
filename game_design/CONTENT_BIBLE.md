# GoalPop Arena Web
## Content Bible

## 1. Art Direction Summary

The game should feel like a playful, toy-like, TV-sports-inspired arcade experience.

### Visual keywords
- bold
- chunky
- playful
- saturated
- readable
- energetic
- exaggerated
- lighthearted competition

### Avoid
- realism
- muddy color palettes
- small unreadable UI
- gritty textures
- direct resemblance to real players or real clubs

---

## 2. Character Art Direction

### Character formula
- oversized head
- compact body
- strong brow / expression
- large shoes or gloves optional
- simplified limbs
- strong outline
- readable front/side profile

### Expression range
Each character should support:
- confident idle
- effort during kick
- surprise during aerial contest
- celebration grin
- defeat slump

### Silhouette rule
Even at small size, characters should be distinguishable by:
- hair shape
- accessory shape
- torso color block
- shoe or glove profile
- special VFX accent color

---

## 3. Originality Guardrails

Do not base characters on:
- named real athletes
- real team kits
- real flags as primary identity markers
- recognizable existing sports-game mascots

Instead use:
- fictional badge shapes
- invented color blocking
- unique hair / visor / band / sleeve motifs
- abstract emblems

## 5. Stadium Art Direction

### Base stadium rules
- audience as stylized clusters
- strong field contrast
- goals visible instantly
- lighting should frame the play area, not wash it out
- the ball must always stand out from the background

### Stadium 1 — Sunset Arena
- warm orange-pink sky
- festive crowd accents
- bright field greens
- golden stadium lights

### Stadium 2 — Neon Night Arena
- cool blue-purple lighting
- sharper highlights
- strong contrast scoreboard
- glowing accent rails

---

## 6. Ball and VFX Direction

### Ball
- simple readable pattern
- strong contrast outline or shading
- optional powered states:
  - fire
  - electric
  - curve trail
  - shield shell

### Goal VFX
- burst ring
- confetti or sparks
- score pop
- brief zoom / shake

### Hit VFX
- dust puff
- speed streak
- star burst
- arc trail

Do not obscure the ball for long.

---

## 7. UI Direction

### Core rules
- large labels
- thick outline
- simple hierarchy
- high contrast
- playful sports-broadcast energy

### Button language
- rounded rectangle or chunky bevel
- obvious hover / pressed states
- large icon support
- color-coded action types

### Suggested colors
- action green
- alert red-orange
- special blue-violet
- neutral slate / charcoal
- off-white text

---

## 8. Typography Direction

Use a blocky, sports-arcade type style.

### Requirements
- strong uppercase support
- readable at small sizes
- heavy weight
- short labels preferred

### In practice
Use:
- PLAY
- REMATCH
- SPECIAL
- GOAL
- WINNER
- SELECT
rather than long paragraphs.

---

## 9. Motion Direction

### General motion
- snappy
- readable
- overshoot on key moments
- no slow realistic easing during gameplay-critical motion

### Best places for juice
- kick contact
- goal scoring
- special activation
- character select confirmation
- results reveal

### Keep subtle
- menu idle animation
- crowd movement
- stadium light sweep

---

## 10. Audio Mood

### Music
- upbeat
- sporty
- slightly goofy
- loop-friendly

### SFX
- punchy
- short
- exaggerated
- layered when needed, but not noisy

---

## 11. Content Expansion Directions

After MVP, good expansion paths:
- new fictional characters
- new stadium themes
- cosmetic ball skins
- alternate special variants
- tournament ladder
- challenge mode

Avoid content expansion that requires legal licensing or heavy live ops.

---

## 12. Packaging Rules for Asset Creation

When generating assets:
- always keep original source art separated from optimized export
- export sprites with consistent naming
- keep character special colors consistent across UI and VFX
- test assets at real phone size before approval

### Naming pattern examples
- `char_blaze_idle_01.png`
- `char_bolt_special_sheet.png`
- `ui_btn_play.png`
- `stadium_sunset_bg.png`
- `vfx_goal_burst_01.png`
